# Theme reference and license notices

Reviewed: 2026-08-16

WebEditor contains 60 canonical research-driven themes and 60 additional
original themes. The canonical themes adapt high-level palette relationships,
surface hierarchy, semantic roles, and accessibility principles into
WebEditor's independent 52-token contract. They are not source-code, pixel, or
token-value copies. The additional 60 themes are original WebEditor palettes
with no third-party reference IDs.

Reference-family names identify design research provenance only. They do not
claim sponsorship, certification, or endorsement. Product branding remains
WebEditor. A source or token change requires this review to be repeated.
